## Does a larger detector improve the final system? (RoBERTa-large swapped into the final pipeline)

*File:* `tables/detector_sensitivity_robertalarge.csv`

> The final two-stage pipeline was run twice on the same 300 conversations, identical except for the detector (RoBERTa-base, 125M parameters, vs RoBERTa-large, 355M).
