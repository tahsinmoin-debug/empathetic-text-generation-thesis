# 3.5 Standards

No engineering standard governs an empathetic dialogue system, so the project followed the published practice that applies to its parts. The data and every model are used under their own licences (Table 3.1), with EmpatheticDialogues restricted to non-commercial research. The response rules follow established guidance on supportive conversation: acknowledging the feeling implements the emotional-reaction mechanism of the EPITOME framework {cite:sharma2020epitome}, and the single-question rule follows counselling guidance against the question-answer trap {cite:samhsa2019tip35}. Empathy is measured with the classifier from the WASSA 2024 shared task {cite:giorgi2024wassa}, and the AI-judge evaluation follows accepted practice: blind scoring, an anchored rubric, temperature 0 and judges from unrelated developers {cite:verga2024}. Statistical claims use paired tests with correction for multiple comparisons (Section 5.4.1). World Health Organization guidance sets the one standard the system does not meet, since it has no crisis safeguard and must not be deployed as a mental-health service {cite:who2023suicide}. The work also follows BRAC University's academic conduct requirements: sources cited, results from the team's own runs, errors reported rather than removed.
